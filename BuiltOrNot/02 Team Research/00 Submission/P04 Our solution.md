**Needs:** The idea in one sentence a non-expert understands; what it does (the core user flow); what will be ready by 11 December; what comes later.
**Rule:** Be clear about what is later.

## Draft
- **One sentence:** BuiltOrNot shows, for every approved solar and battery site in Romania, whether it is really being built, with dates from Sentinel-2 and the paperwork behind it.
- **What it does:**
  1. Search a company, CUI or county.
  2. Open a project page: grid approval, permits and CfD award, a Sentinel-2 time-lapse, and the detected stage with dates and confidence.
  3. Zoom out to a county map of approved against built.
- **Ready by 11 December** (the Sentinel-2 part of the v1.1 build list):
  - National map with county aggregates.
  - 30 hand-linked projects with pages and time-lapse.
  - Site clearing and modules detected and dated, with an "unsure" class.
  - An evidence change log.
  - Mock-ups of Build Signals (3 real examples) and the correction channel.
- **Later:**
  - MWp and pile stage from paid very-high-resolution imagery.
  - BESS container counts.
  - The Build-out Feed for traders.
  - Deadline Radar bands.
  - An API.
