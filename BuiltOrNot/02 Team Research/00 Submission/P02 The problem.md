**Needs:** An image or map of the problem area; the problem in one or two sentences; where it happens and who is affected; one number that shows the scale, with its source.
**Rule:** Show a real problem, where it happens and who suffers from it.

## Draft
- **What is the problem?** Romania has approved far more grid connections than it is building, and public records cannot tell which approved solar and battery projects are really under construction.
- **Where, and who is affected?** All of Romania: more than 1,400 grid approvals (ATRs) held by more than 1,000 companies ([[Sources#^S4|S4]], [[Sources#^S5|S5]]). Affected: investors and lenders deciding on projects, traders forecasting supply, Transelectrica and ANRE trying to free blocked capacity, and real developers waiting for grid space.
- **Why it matters (one number). Options:**
  - **A (recommended):** more than 80,000 MW approved (Government of Romania, 25 Apr 2026) ([[Sources#^S5|S5]]), against 2,514 MW actually connected in 2026 up to 20 Aug (Transelectrica) ([[Sources#^S25|S25]]). Both are official sources.
  - **B:** only about 1% of projects that posted a guarantee also held an establishment authorisation (ANRE, data at 1 Jan 2026) ([[Sources#^S8|S8]]).
- **Image:** a map of approved MW by county from the ATR list.
