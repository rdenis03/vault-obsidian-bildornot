**Question.** Which rules force ATR holders to build or let go, and by when?

## ANRE Order 15/2026
Adopted 2026-05-21, published in Monitorul Oficial no. 436, in force 2026-05-25 ([[Sources#^S10|S10]], [[Sources#^S11|S11]], [[Sources#^S12|S12]]).

| Rule | Deadline | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| ATR guarantee rises from 5% to 20% of the connection tariff | At ATR issue | ([[Sources#^S11\|S11]]) |
| Establishment authorisation (AI) within 12 months of the connection contract or 18 months of the ATR | 12 / 18 months | ([[Sources#^S11\|S11]], [[Sources#^S15\|S15]]) |
| One extension of up to 12 months | +12 months | ([[Sources#^S13\|S13]], [[Sources#^S14\|S14]]) |
| Extra 5% guarantee per 12-month extension | Per extension | ([[Sources#^S10\|S10]]) |
| Connection contracts already in force get 12 months from entry into force | ~2027-05-25 (ESTIMATE, computed) | ([[Sources#^S11\|S11]], [[Sources#^S15\|S15]]) |
| Withdrawal of the AI ends the ATR; the guarantee is executed | On withdrawal | ([[Sources#^S9\|S9]]) |

## Other instruments

- **Single source:** a 2026-07-14 deposit deadline (Momentum Group, last-resort source) ([[Sources#^S16|S16]]).

## Why it matters
- The window to about May 2027 is when holders, buyers and lenders most need dated evidence of physical progress. Sell into it (evidence-pack recommendation).
- v1.1 treats this as the launch hook, not the business: the 12-month rule purges phantom projects by about 2027–28 ([[Business model v1.1]], change 6).
