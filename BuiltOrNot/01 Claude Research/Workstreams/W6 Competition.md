**Question.** Who already monitors solar and BESS construction from space, where, and for whom?

## Findings
- **Kayrros** sells Solar and Battery Storage Construction Monitors with weekly forecast updates for ERCOT and CAISO ([[Sources#^S81|S81]], [[Sources#^S82|S82]], [[Sources#^S83|S83]]), and an investor-facing renewables monitor with weekly construction updates ([[Sources#^S84|S84]], [[Sources#^B1|B1]]). Its site announces that Energy Aspects completed an acquisition of Kayrros (date not found) ([[Sources#^S95|S95]]).
- **Zeitview** reports construction progress from drones and aircraft, claims more than 300 GW scanned, and names Primergy as a customer ([[Sources#^S85|S85]]).
- **LiveEO** focuses on grid vegetation ([[Sources#^S86|S86]]).
- From the v1.1 review (v1.0 sources and course material):
  - i-EM monitors plant construction for Enel.
  - GEOSAT works in solar.
  - Lium monitors construction in the US on PlanetScope daily imagery plus SkySat tasking ([[Sources#^B2|B2]]).
  - ESA funded ConsTrack, construction monitoring for banks (GeoVille, completed 2018) ([[Sources#^B3|B3]]).
- **No CEE coverage found** for any of them.

## Positioning
The honest claim is not "nobody tracks this" but "nobody does it for Romania with the paperwork joined". Watch Kayrros: if it enters CEE, the moat must already be the truth table and lender acceptance ([[Business model v1.1]], weak point 9).

## Update 2026-11-07
- **COTESA for EDP (Portugal):** a EUSPA-supported proof of concept that detects PV construction start and progress from Sentinel-2 time series, with change detection and AI models, over 15 km² of 2022–24 builds. It reports the method works above a 150 m² project size, with super-resolution and VHR as options ([[Sources#^S107|S107]]). This is the closest Sentinel-2 precedent found; no product or pricing is published.
